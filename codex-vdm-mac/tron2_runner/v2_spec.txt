
TRON2 specifications and coordinate contracts:
- The controller's left wrist pitch and roll limits are both [-0.7505, +0.7505] radians (about ±43 degrees). The local planner reserves 0.006 radians inside each limit.
- `get_left_ee_pose()` and `goto_pose_left()` use the same fixed tool origin (TCP). XYZ is in meters in `base_Link`. The WXYZ quaternion represents the rotation `R` from the tool frame into `base_Link`.
- In `base_Link`, the finger direction is `R @ [0, 0, -1]` and the signed jaw axis is `R @ [0, 1, 0]`. Reversing `jaw_direction` rotates the tool by 180 degrees about the unchanged finger direction.
- `make_grasp_pose()` uses a fixed pad midpoint calibrated at a 50 mm jaw opening. Its offset from the TCP is approximately `[0.00214, 0, -0.01509]` m in the tool frame. In `base_Link`, the nominal pad midpoint is `tcp_xyz + R @ offset`. The conversion to `tcp_xyz` already accounts for this offset. The physical pad midpoint moves as the jaws open.
- The simulated jaw collision mesh extends about 50 mm from the TCP along tool local `-Z` at full opening and about 54 mm at a 50 mm opening. Relative to the fixed nominal pad reference, those axial projections are about 34 mm and 39 mm. For a tilted tool, the height of a mesh point in `base_Link` also depends on its lateral offset.
